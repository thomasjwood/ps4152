1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: ABC News/CBS News/NBC News/CNN
 Conducted by Voter Research & Surveys, November 3, 1992 and based on
 self-administered interviews with 15490 exiting voters.
  
Approximate number of variables: 114
Topical Coverage: 
 Vote for president 1988 and 1992(2); economic situation(2); candidates
 qualities(1); vote for senator, representative and governor, if
 applicable(2); abortion(1); role of government(5); important issues in
 vote decision(2); taxes(5).
