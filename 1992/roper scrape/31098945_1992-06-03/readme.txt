1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Washington Post, June 3-7, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with a national adult sample of 1005.
  
Approximate number of variables: 31
Topical Coverage: 
 Knowledge of Watergate (1); Watergate (3); effect of Watergate on
 government, ethics and public life (2).
