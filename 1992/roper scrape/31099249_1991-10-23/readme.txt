1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, October 23, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 500.
  
Approximate number of variables: 32
Topical Coverage: 
 Party preference (1); national situation (1); taxes (1); individual
 privacy (9); alternative medicine (9); UFOs (2).
