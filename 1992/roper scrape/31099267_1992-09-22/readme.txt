1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, September 22-24, 1992 and
 based on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1400.
  
Approximate number of variables: 57
Topical Coverage: 
 Party preference (1); national situation (1); voter registration (1);
 voting intentions (7); presidential candidates (12); George Bush job
 performance (1); opinion of political leaders (1); voting record (1);
 policy preferences (2); environment (7); political trust (8); POW/MIA
 (3); Christopher Columbus (2); Fidel Castro (1).
