1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, April 15-16, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1006.
  
Approximate number of variables: 28
Topical Coverage: 
 Vote for President in three-way election (2); support for Ross Perot
 (2); effect of certain stands by the candidates on vote (7); importance
 of major party President (1); participation in Perot's campaign (2).
