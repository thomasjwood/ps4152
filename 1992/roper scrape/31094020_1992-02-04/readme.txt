1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Star Tribune, February 4-8, 1992 and based on
 telephone (landline only) interviews with 600 adult residents of
 Minnesota.
  
Approximate number of variables: 51
Topical Coverage: 
 Daily assistance (8); elderly quality of life (1); institutional
 assistance (6); problems of the elderly (7); elderly parenthood (10);
 caregivers (6).
