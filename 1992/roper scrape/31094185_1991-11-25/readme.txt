1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Americans Talk Issues Foundation
 Conducted by Market Strategies, Inc., November 25-December 2, 1991 and
 based on telephone (landline only) interviews with a national adult
 sample of 1410.
  
Approximate number of variables: 146
Topical Coverage: 
 Direction of country (1); direction of world (1); Soviet Union treat to
 United States (1); trusting USSR (2); threat of emerging Soviet
 republics (1); Cold War over or not (1); reason for reduced Soviet
 expansion (1); effect of communism on US military (1); starting war in
 Iraq as right/wrong (1); agreement with Iraq war statements (6);
 military presence in Middle East (1); globalization as positive or
 negative (2); business practices needed regulation (7); actions US and
 United Nations should try to stop (6); effective ways to deal with
 dictators (27); actions warranting military intervention (12);
 agreement with statements about various dictators (18); UN job
 performance (11); view of UN World Court (1); support for potential UN
 changes (3); following UN resolutions instead of own laws (16); UN
 resolutions having force of law (4); fairness of UN vote system (1); US
 paying back (1); presidential candidate preference (6); voter
 likelihood (1).
