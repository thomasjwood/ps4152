1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 November 12-19, 1991 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews
 with 737 adult residents of Connecticut. There are three
 versions of the questionnaire: 1) Version A--Asked November 12-13,
 1991, 2) Version B--Asked November 14-17, 1991 and 3) Version C—Asked
 November 14-19, 1991.
  
Approximate number of variables: 59
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); constitutional convention to deal with
 modern issues (2); Bill of Rights (3); president suspension of
 Constitution in times of war (1); Constitution (9); Connecticut
 politicians (5); job performance of Senator Christopher Dodd (1);
 Governor Lowell Weicker/General Assembly job performance (2); state
 income tax (5); state budget (2); AIDS (23).
