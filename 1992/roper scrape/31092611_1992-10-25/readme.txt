1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Des Moines Register, October 25-30, 1992 and based on
 telephone (landline only) interviews with a Likely voters of Iowa
 sample of 837.
  
Approximate number of variables: 40
Topical Coverage: 
 1992 presidential election (14); congressional election (2); senatorial
 election (1); equal rights amendment to the Iowa Constitution (3).
