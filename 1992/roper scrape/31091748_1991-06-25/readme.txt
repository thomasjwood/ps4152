1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 June 25-July 1, 1991 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews
 with 500 adult residents of Connecticut.
  
Approximate number of variables: 76
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (2); satisfaction with personal life (2);
 U.S. role in world (6); democracy (4); Governor Lowell Weicker job
 performance (3); low-level radioactive disposal sites and risks (17);
 abortion (12); movie ratings (15).
