1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, October 22-23, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 885.
  
Approximate number of variables: 27
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (2); party preference (2); voting intentions (4);
 presidential candidates (12); George Bush job performance (1); economy
 (1).
