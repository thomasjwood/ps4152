1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Center for Public Policy, Brown University, June 07-11,
 1992 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews with 638
 registered voters in Rhode Island.
  
Approximate number of variables: 44
Topical Coverage: 
 1988 presidential election (1); vote in upcoming elections given
 certain candidates (4); vote in primary given certain candidates and
 positions (4); term limits (2); Rhode Island on right track (1); Rhode
 Island economy (3); personal financial situation (2); state business
 conditions (1); good time to buy (1); George Bush job performance (1);
 John Chafee job performance (1); Claiborne Pell job performance (1);
 Ron Machtley job performance (1); Jack Reed job performance (1); Bruce
 Sundlun job performance (1); Rhode Island most important problem (1);
 Rhode Island budget deficit (8).
