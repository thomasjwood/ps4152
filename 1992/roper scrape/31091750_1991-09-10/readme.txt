1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 September 10-16, 1991 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews
 with an adult residents of Connecticut sample of 944. There are three
 separate data files for this study: 1) Version A--September 10 N=56, 2)
 Version B--September 11-16 N=444 and 3) Version C--September 14, 16-24
 N=444.
  
Approximate number of variables: 87
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (3); Soviet Union (10); defense spending
 (3); Connecticut cities (20); Clarence Thomas and the Supreme Court
 (12); Senator Joe Lieberman and Senator Christopher Dodd job
 performance (3); Governor Lowell Weicker job performance (2); state
 legislature job performance (1); state taxes and spending (16).
