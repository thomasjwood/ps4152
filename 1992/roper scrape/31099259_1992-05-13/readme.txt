1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, May 13-14, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1250.
  
Approximate number of variables: 48
Topical Coverage: 
 Party preference (1); national situation (1); most important issue (1);
 voter registration (1); evaluation of government performance (2);
 voting intention (3); opinion of political leaders (3); candidates (6);
 George W. Bush job performance (2); welfare (6); urban violence (3);
 terrorism (2); personal goals (1).
