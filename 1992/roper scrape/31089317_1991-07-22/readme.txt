1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Advisory Commission on Intergovernmental Relations
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, July 22-25, 1991 and based on
 personal interviews with a national adult sample of 1069.
  
Approximate number of variables: 45
Topical Coverage: 
 Type of tax that is least fair (1); which level of government get the
 most for your money (2); government cooperation (1); role of federal
 government (6); perception of the distribution of power between the
 levels of government (4).
