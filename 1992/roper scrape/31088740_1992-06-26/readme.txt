1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, June 26-July 1, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1206.
  
Approximate number of variables: 57
Topical Coverage: 
 1992 election (8); George Bush job performance (1); honesty and ethical
 standards of people in various occupations (25); candidates' economic
 plans (2); Ross Perot (3); Bush and Perot media accusations (3).
