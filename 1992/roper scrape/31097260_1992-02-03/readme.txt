1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Center for Public Policy, Brown University, February 03-
 05, 1992 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews with 545
 registered voters in Rhode Island.
  
Approximate number of variables: 42
Topical Coverage: 
 Vote in presidential primary (2); Rhode Island economy (2); personal
 financial situation (2); George Bush job performance (1); John Chafee
 job performance (1); Claiborne Pell job performance (1); Bruce Sundlun
 job performance (1); Roger Begin job performance (1); James O'Neil job
 performance (1); Kathleen Connell job performance (1); Anthony Solomon
 job performance (1); Joseph DeAngelis job performance (1); John
 Bevilacqua job performance (1); Rhode Island General Assembly job
 performance (1); party preferred to control state legislature (1);
 opinion of Frank Flaherty (1); opinion of Micheal Levesque (1); opinion
 of John Holmes (1); vote for governor given specific candidates (4);
 likelihood of voting in upcoming state primary (1); vote for candidates
 given specific people and positions (3); news viewing (3).
