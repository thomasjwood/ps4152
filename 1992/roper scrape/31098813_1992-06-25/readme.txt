1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by WABC-TV and NEWSDAY, June 25-28, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with 755 adult residents of New York State.
  
Approximate number of variables: 29
Topical Coverage: 
 State government performance (1); voting record (1); voting intentions
 (6); candidates issues (14); party preference (2); voter registration
 (1).
