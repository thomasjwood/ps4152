1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, October 10, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 500.
  
Approximate number of variables: 51
Topical Coverage: 
 National situation (1); George Bush job performance (1); Clarence
 Thomas nomination (1); condom distribution in schools (2); influence of
 religion in American life and in elections (8); religion (5); religion
 in schools  (2); sexual harassment (3); definition of sexual harassment
 (7); experience with sexual harassment (2); Judge Thomas and the sexual
 harassment issue (5).
