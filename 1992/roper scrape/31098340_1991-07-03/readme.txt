1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: USA Today
 Conducted by Gordon S. Black Corporation, July 3, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with 402 Black adults who heard of Clarence Thomas'
 Supreme Court nomination.
  
Approximate number of variables: 20
Topical Coverage: 
 Awareness of Clarence Thomas nomination to Supreme Court (1); Thomas
 nomination (7).
