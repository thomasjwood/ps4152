1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by CBS News, July 14-15, 1992 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of 1278.
  
Approximate number of variables: 39
Topical Coverage: 
 Voting intention (5); voter registration (1); voting record (3);party
 preference (1); ideology (1); opinion of political leaders (3); support
 for candidates (1); candidates (10); election (1); political parties
 (3).
