1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: U.S. News and World Report
 Conducted by Princeton Survey Research Associates, January 18-21, 1992
 and based on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1500.
  
Approximate number of variables: 73
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); presidential campaign (2); problems,
 finances and family (32); jobs (33); home ownership (5).
