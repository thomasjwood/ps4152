1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Washington Post, October 21-25, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1001.
  
Approximate number of variables: 60
Topical Coverage: 
 Intention to vote (3); 1992 presidential trial heat (11); confidence in
 the federal government (6).
