1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: ABC News / The Washington Post
 Conducted by ICR Survey Research Group, September 16-20, 1992 and based
 on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1003.
  
Approximate number of variables: 48
Topical Coverage: 
 Intention to vote (3); 1992 presidential trial heat (5); important
 issues in vote decision (3).
