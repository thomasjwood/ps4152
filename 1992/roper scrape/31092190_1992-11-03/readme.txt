1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Henry J. Kaiser Family Foundation & the Harvard School of Public Health
 Conducted by Louis Harris and Associates, November 3, 1992 and based on
 not available interviews with 1479 adults nationwide who said they voted
 in the 1992 election.
  
Approximate number of variables: 106
Topical Coverage: 
 Most important election issues (2); most important reason for candidate
 preference (1); most important issues for new presidents (2); major
 changes to healthcare system (3); ways to control health care costs
 (3); additional spending on national health insurance (12); health
 insurance coverage (1); Health Maintenance Organization--HMO membership
 (1).
