1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Des Moines Register, October 4-9, 1992 and based on
 telephone (landline only) interviews with 514 Likely voters in
 Congressional District 2, Iowa.
  
Approximate number of variables: 25
Topical Coverage: 
 Congressional candidate preference (1); opinion on abortion (1).
