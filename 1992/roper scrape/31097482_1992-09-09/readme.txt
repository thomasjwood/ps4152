1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Roper Organization, September 9-16, 1992 and based on
 face to face interviews with a national adult sample of 1993.
  
Approximate number of variables: 79
Topical Coverage: 
 National economic situation (2); national situation (2); personal
 economic situation (2); environment (11); personal stress (2); credit
 cards (4); warehouse shopping clubs (9); personal computer use (4);
 crime (11); political leaders (3); ideology (1); party preference (1).
