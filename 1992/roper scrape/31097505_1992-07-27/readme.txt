1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: American Jewish Committee
 Conducted by The Roper Organization, July 27-August 10, 1992 and based
 on telephone interviews with 1057 adult residents of New York City, New
 York.
  
Approximate number of variables: 93
Topical Coverage: 
 Quality of life in New York City (2); problems in the city (5);
 immigrants (2); relations between ethnic and racial groups (2); insults
 from different groups (1); media (1); influence in New York City (6);
 treatment of ethnic/racial groups by the government (2); hostile groups
 (6); discrimination (7); stereotypes (36); ethnic/racial groups as
 neighbors (6); religion (1).
