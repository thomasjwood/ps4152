1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, October 28-29, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1217.
  
Approximate number of variables: 33
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (2); voting intentions (8); party preference (2);
 voting record (3); presidential candidates (11).
