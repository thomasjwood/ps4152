1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Star Tribune, January 2-11, 1992 and based on
 telephone (landline only) interviews with 800 adult residents of
 Minnesota.
  
Approximate number of variables: 49
Topical Coverage: 
 Local situation (1); George Bush job performance (2); Persian Gulf war
 (1); state political leaders (14); voting intention (1); Senator Dave
 Durenberger misconduct (5); Super Bowl (10); sports preference (2).
