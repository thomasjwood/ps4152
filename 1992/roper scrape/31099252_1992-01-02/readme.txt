1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, January 2, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 500.
  
Approximate number of variables: 77
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (5); 1992 presidential election (7);
 economy (18); United States-Japan trade relations (6); environment
 (15).
