1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Des Moines Register, June 14-18 and 22-23, 1992 and
 based on telephone (landline only) interviews with 233 likely voters in
 the Second Congressional District, Iowa. The sample is an
 oversample for study # USIOWA1992-318.
  
Approximate number of variables: 16
Topical Coverage: 
 Congressional candidate preferences (1); abortion (1); presidential
 campaign interest (1); overdrafts (1).
