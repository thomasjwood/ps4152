1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, July 11-14, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1217.
  
Approximate number of variables: 51
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); problems facing the U.S. (2); Bush's
 foreign policy in South Africa and Middle East (7); personal financial
 situation (3); economic conditions in the country (3); Supreme Court
 rulings over past two years (4); Supreme Court nominees (2); Clarence
 Thomas (6); abortion (2); police brutality (4); health care (3).
