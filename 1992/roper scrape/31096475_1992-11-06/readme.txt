1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Prevention Magazine
 Conducted by Princeton Survey Research Associates, November 6- December
 4, 1992 and based on telephone interviews with a national adult sample
 of 1250.
  
Approximate number of variables: 77
Topical Coverage: 
 Personal health record (14); smoking (3); alcohol (2); driving habits
 (4); health insurance record (8); auto safety (7); use of medicine
 (16); medication and children (5); AIDS (8).
