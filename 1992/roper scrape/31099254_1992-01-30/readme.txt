1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, January 30, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1000.
  
Approximate number of variables: 57
Topical Coverage: 
 National situation (1); voter registration (1); party preference (2);
 voting intention (3); political leaders (1); presidential candidates
 (4); role of the press (6); George Bush job performance (1); economic
 issues (4); knowledge of Japan (3); Japan (18).
