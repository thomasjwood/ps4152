1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 September 29-October 5, 1992 and based on telephone (landline only)
 interviews with 553 adult residents of Connecticut.
  
Approximate number of variables: 71
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); party preference (1); political
 ideology (1); presidential candidates (14); voting intentions (5);
 state political leaders (13); economic situation (3); national
 situation (3); Christopher Columbus (6); ethnic background (2); casino
 gambling (13); union membership (1); voter registration (2).
