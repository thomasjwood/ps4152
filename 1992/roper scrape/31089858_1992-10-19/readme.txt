1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: WSB-TV
 Conducted by The Atlanta Journal-Constitution, October 19-21, 1992 and
 based on telephone interviews with 832 adult residents of Georgia.
  
Approximate number of variables: 47
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (1); voting intentions (7); political interest (1);
 Bush job performance (2); political leaders (5); economic situation
 (2); presidential candidates (1); women in politics (6); local
 authorities job performance (3); public education (2); voting record
 (1); party preference (1); political ideology (1); church attendance
 (1).
