1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Times Mirror Center for the People & the Press
 Conducted by Princeton Survey Research Associates, July 29-August 1,
 1992 and based on telephone interviews with 1023 registered voter callback.
 Respondent were originally interviewed May 28-June 10, 1992.
  
Approximate number of variables: 28
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); frequent news interest (3); Ross Perot
 (1); voter interest and involvement (9); George Bush vs. Bill Clinton
 (8); Bush vs. Clinton vs. Perot (2): Bush vs. Clinton vs. Norman
 Schwartzkopf (2); satisfaction with candidates (2); voter registration
 (4); reasons not to vote (6); politics as a career (1); interest in
 politics (1); effectiveness of government (5); strong leadership (1);
 vote of no confidence (1); importance of who is elected (1); Senate
 majority (1); House of representatives majority (1); communist civil
 war (1); television station Dan Quayle criticized (1); news sources
 (4); watching events on television (57); opinion of certain people (8);
 opinion of certain statements (60); things that keep respondent from
 news (2); time engaged in news (3); reading certain publications (5);
 watching certain programs (8); reading books (2); political involvement
 (8); opinion of groups or people (15); suggesting candidate opinion and
 personality (2); George Bush (7); Bill Clinton (6); opinion of
 candidates (4); Bush/Colin Powell vs. Clinton/Al Gore (1); abortion
 (1).
