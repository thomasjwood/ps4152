1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, February 28-March 1, 1992 and based
 on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1001.
  
Approximate number of variables: 55
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (4); presidential candidates (13);
 presidential campaign (1); economic and tax proposals (9); silicone
 breast implant controversy (3); crime (4).
