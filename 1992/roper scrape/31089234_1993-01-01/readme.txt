1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Employee Benefit Research Institute
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, January, 1993 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of 1000.
  
Approximate number of variables: 20
Topical Coverage: 
 Retirement plans (13).
