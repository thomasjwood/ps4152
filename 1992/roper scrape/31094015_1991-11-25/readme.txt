1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Star Tribune, November 25-27, 1991 and based on
 telephone (landline only) interviews with 604 adult residents of
 Minnesota.
  
Approximate number of variables: 10
Topical Coverage: 
 Minnesota businesses (5); Northwest airlines proposal (5).
