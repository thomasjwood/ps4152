1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, June 13-16, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a National adult sample plus an oversample of
 African Americans sample of 990.
  
Approximate number of variables: 61
Topical Coverage: 
 Bush job performance(2); civil rights progress(2); opportunities for
 blacks(4); civil rights legislation(2); bills on civil rights in
 Congress(4); ways to help the Black community(6); racism in the work
 place(3); racism(2); death penalty(8).
