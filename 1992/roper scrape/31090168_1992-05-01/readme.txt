1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Associated Press
 Conducted by ICR Survey Research Group, May 1-5, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1006.
  
Approximate number of variables: 31
Topical Coverage: 
 Crime (1); police treatment of all groups (2); frequency of area police
 use of unjustified force (1); Los Angeles riots (1); Rodney King and
 the police officers' trial (1).
