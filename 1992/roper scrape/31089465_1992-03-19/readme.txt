1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, March 19-20, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 981.
  
Approximate number of variables: 22
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); presidential election (14);
 characteristics of presidential candidates (7).
