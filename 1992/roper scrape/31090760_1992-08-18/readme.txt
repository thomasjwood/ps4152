1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by CBS News, August 18-19, 1992 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of 1546.
  
Approximate number of variables: 40
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (3); voting intention (4); voter
 registration (1); voting record (1); party preference (2); ideology
 (1); opinion of political leader (4); support for candidates (1);
 candidates (9); political parties (4); family values (2).
