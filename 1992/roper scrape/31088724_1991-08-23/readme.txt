1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, August 23-25, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1018.
  
Approximate number of variables: 56
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance( 4); state of country (1); Bush in 1992
 election (9); 1992 Democrat possibilities (5); Mikhail Gorbachev (2);
 Soviet Union (3); Boris Yeltsin (2); Soviet Union & U.S./nuclear arms
 reduction (14).
