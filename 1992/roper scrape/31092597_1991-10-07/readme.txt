1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Des Moines Register, October 7-10, 13-16, 1991 and
 based on telephone (landline only) interviews with 802 adult residents of
 Iowa.
  
Approximate number of variables: 81
Topical Coverage: 
 Favorability of politicians (13); politician job performances (5); Tom
 Harkin as president (5); George Bush job performance (9); legalized
 gambling (6); state lottery (1); university budget cuts (5); university
 salaries (1); Soviet Union changes (2); Soviet leaders (1); Soviet
 economic aid (1); laying off state employees (3); county reductions
 (1); eliminating road spending (1); voter spending approval (1);
 defense spending (1); nuclear arsenal reduction (1); people in space
 (1); Mars civilian trips (1); Iowa economy (1); household pests (7).
