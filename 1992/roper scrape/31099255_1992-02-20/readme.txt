1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, February 20, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1250.
  
Approximate number of variables: 92
Topical Coverage: 
 National situation (1); vote situation in the Republican primaries (2);
 vote for President (6); George Bush job performance (4); impressions of
 Democratic candidates (6); vote in Democratic Primary (2); impressions
 of Republican candidates (7); impression of Hillary Clinton (2); major
 problems facing women today (15); women's movement (8); women and
 society (6); gender roles (2).
