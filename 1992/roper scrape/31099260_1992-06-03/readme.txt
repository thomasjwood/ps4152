1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, June 3-4, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1250.
  
Approximate number of variables: 64
Topical Coverage: 
 Party preference (2); national situation (1); voter registration (1);
 voting intentions (4); presidential candidates (12); George Bush job
 performance (1); opinion of political leaders (3); presidential
 campaign (6); political parties (6); abortion (2); religion (12).
