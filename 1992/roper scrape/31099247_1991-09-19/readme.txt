1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, September 19-20, 1991 and
 based on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 500.
  
Approximate number of variables: 64
Topical Coverage: 
 National situation (1); George Bush job performance (3); government
 spending (7); U.S. involvement in Iraq (2); U.S. loan guarantee to
 Israel (2); U.S. foreign policy (12); Soviet Union (4); U.S.-Soviet
 relations (10); sexual discrimination (3).
