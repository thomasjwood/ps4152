1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Washington Post, August 20-21, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with a national adult sample of 1017.
  
Approximate number of variables: 38
Topical Coverage: 
 1992 presidential election (16); presidential candidates (12); George
 Bush job performance (7); Republican convention (3).
