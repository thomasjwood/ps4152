1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Atlanta Journal Constitution
 Conducted by Voice Information Services, Inc., June 9-12, 1991 and
 based on telephone interviews with 1048 adult residents of Metro-Altanta,
 Georgia. The sample includes respondents from Clayton, Cobb, DeKalb, 
 Fulton and Gwinnett counties.
  
Approximate number of variables: 53
Topical Coverage: 
 Discrimination in Atlanta (1); integration (1); favor/oppose making it
 illegal to deny housing or a job to homosexuals (2); homosexual
 relations in Georgia (3); vote for gay candidate (1); homosexuals in
 specific occupations (6); effect of gay community on Atlanta (2).
