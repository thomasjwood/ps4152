1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 July 23-29, 1991 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews with
 an adult residents of Connecticut sample of 500.
  
Approximate number of variables: 83
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (2); Senator Joe Lieberman and Senator
 Christopher Dodd job performance (2); looking back at the war in the
 Persian Gulf (12); Governor Lowell Weicker job performance (3); state
 legislature job performance (1); state taxes and spending (23); women,
 men and work (12); women in combat (5); sports (10).
