1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: USA Today
 Conducted by Gordon S. Black Corporation, May 26-June 9, 1992 and based
 on telephone interviews with 750 campaign contributors--350 Bush Campaign Contributors and 400
 Clinton Campaign Contributors.
  
Approximate number of variables: 38
Topical Coverage: 
 Party preference (2); voter registration (1); candidates issues (5);
 campaign contributions (13); voting intentions (3); political
 responsiveness (2); national issues (4); union membership (1).
