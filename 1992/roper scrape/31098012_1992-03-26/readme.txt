1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Times Mirror Center for the People & the Press
 Conducted by Princeton Survey Research Associates, March 26-29, 1992
 and based on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1668.
 There are 3 forms for this study located in column 168.  Form 1=1; Form
 2=2, Form 3=3.
  
Approximate number of variables: 152
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (2); Congressional job performance (1);
 most important news event (1); following recent news stories (16);
 rating press (1); best news network (1); press covering presidential
 campaign (1); voter interest and involvement (2); presidential
 primaries (1); bias of news organizations (2); Apartheid in South
 Africa (1); knowledge of candidates (6); Bill Clinton (7); economic
 conditions (2); news source for presidential campaign (1); Democratic
 candidate (2); George Bush vs. Bill Clinton (7); George Bush vs. Jerry
 Brown (2); satisfaction with candidates (2); press coverage of
 candidates (3); George Bush (3); Bush vs. Clinton on personality traits
 (9); candidate most likely to win (1); check bouncing scandal (6);
 opinion of certain people (5); opinion on certain statements (17).
