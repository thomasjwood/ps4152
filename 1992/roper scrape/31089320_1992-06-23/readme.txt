1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Advisory Commission on Intergovernmental Relations
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, June 23-28, 1992 and based on face to
 face interviews with a national adult sample of 1045.
  
Approximate number of variables: 25
Topical Coverage: 
 Party preference (2); television viewership (3); union membership (1);
 evaluation of tax system (2); public service (2); evaluation of
 government (9).
