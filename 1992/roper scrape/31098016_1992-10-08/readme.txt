1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Times Mirror Center for the People & the Press
 Conducted by Princeton Survey Research Associates, October 8-11, 1992
 and based on telephone interviews with a Registered voter sample of
 1815.
  
Approximate number of variables: 96
Topical Coverage: 
 Voting/registration habits (4); 1992 presidential election (45);
 political beliefs (17).
