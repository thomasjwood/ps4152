1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Center for Public Policy, Brown University, September 20-
 23, 1992 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews with 516
 registered voters in Rhode Island.
  
Approximate number of variables: 33
Topical Coverage: 
 1988 presidential election (1); vote in upcoming elections given
 specific candidates and positions (6); term limits (2); Bruce Sundlun
 job performance (2); Elizabeth Leonard job performance (1); Rhode
 Island on right track (1); Rhode Island economy (2); personal financial
 situation (2); state business conditions (1); good time to buy (1);
 legalized gambling in Rhode Island (2); George Bush job performance
 (1); John Chafee job performance (1); Claiborne Pell job performance
 (1); Rhode Island most important problem (1).
