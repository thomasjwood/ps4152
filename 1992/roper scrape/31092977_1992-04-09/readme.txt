1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Los Angeles Times, April 9-15, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with 2619 adult residents of Los Angeles, Orange, San Diego,
 Riverside, San Bernardino and Ventura Counties.
  
Approximate number of variables: 52
Topical Coverage: 
 Ownership of firearms (23); problems facing the community (1); state of
 economy (4); personal finances (1); death penalty (1); crime and safety
 (3); gun-related crimes (12); use of firearms (5); politics in Southern
 California (2).
