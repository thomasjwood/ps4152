1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Star Tribune, October 2-6, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with 830 adult residents of Minnesota.
  
Approximate number of variables: 55
Topical Coverage: 
 National situation (1); voter registration (1); voting intention (9);
 political interest (1); state political leaders (5); candidates (19);
 George Bush job performance (2); political cynicism (2); media coverage
 (3); personal economic situation (1); local economic situation (1);
 party preference (2); ideology (1).
