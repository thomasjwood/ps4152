1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, October 10-11, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 704.
  
Approximate number of variables: 32
Topical Coverage: 
 Women's movement, feminism (8); sexual harassment (14); Clarence Thomas
 /Anita Hill (5).
