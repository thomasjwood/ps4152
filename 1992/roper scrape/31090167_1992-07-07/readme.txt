1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Associated Press
 Conducted by ICR Survey Research Group, July 7, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1007.
  
Approximate number of variables: 29
Topical Coverage: 
 Interest in the Olympics (1); commercialization of the Olympics (1);
 International Olympic Committee (2); professional athletes on the U.S.
 Olympic team (1).
