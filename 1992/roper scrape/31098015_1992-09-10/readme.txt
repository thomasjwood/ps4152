1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Times Mirror Center for the People & the Press
 Conducted by Princeton Survey Research Associates, September 10-13,
 1992 and based on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of
 1508. Most questions were asked of 1,175 registered voters.  There are
 2 forms for this study located in column 146.  Form 1=1; Form 2=2.
  
Approximate number of variables: 128
Topical Coverage: 
 Life satisfaction (1); most important news event (1); following news
 stories (18); press job performance (2); news network job performance
 (1); news sources (2); Senate majority (1); House of Representatives
 majority (1); ethnic conflict in Bosnia (1); voter interest and
 involvement (4); George Bush/Dan Quayle vs. Bill Clinton/Al Gore (2);
 George Bush (3); Bill Clinton (5); describing presidential candidates
 (9); grading candidate campaigns (4); rating ideology (4); campaign
 news stories (11); Hillary Clinton (1); Al Gore (1); cause of lack of
 progress (1); poverty (1); news coverage of election (1); influence of
 news organizations (1); press covering candidates (2); press preference
 for president (2); Ross Perot (2); opinion of certain statements (17).
