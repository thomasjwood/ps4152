1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Des Moines Register, October 25-30, 1992 and based on
 telephone (landline only) interviews with a Likely voters residing in
 the Second Congressional District of Iowa sample of 614.
  
Approximate number of variables: 22
Topical Coverage: 
 Congressional District 2 election (2).
