1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Nightly Business Report News Network & Reuters News Service
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, January 4-13, 1993 and based
 on telephone interviews with 359 executives of American companies with
 more than $1 million in sales or revenue.
  
Approximate number of variables: 66
Topical Coverage: 
 Impression of Bill Clinton (3); important problems facing the country
 (6); economic stimulus (7); impressions of Clinton administration
 appointees (9); economic situation (15).
