1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: USA Today
 Conducted by Gordon S. Black Corporation, July 31-August 1, 1992 and
 based on telephone interviews with 602 adults who have watched a portion
 of the Olympics.
  
Approximate number of variables: 22
Topical Coverage: 
 Olympics viewership (1); "triple cast" (1); rate NBC coverage and
 commentators (13); professional athletes in the Olympics (1); fall of
 communism and the Olympics (1).
