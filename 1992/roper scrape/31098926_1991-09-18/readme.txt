1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Washington Post, September 18-22, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1000.
  
Approximate number of variables: 15
Topical Coverage: 
 Voting intentions (1); ideology (1); party identification (1).
