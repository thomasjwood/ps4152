1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Cable News Network and USA Today
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, January 6-9, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with 1421 National adults plus an oversample of 206
 Black adults.
  
Approximate number of variables: 73
Topical Coverage: 
 The 1992 election and candidates (7); vote for George Bush or a
 Democratic challenger (4); Bush job performance (1); Congressional job
 performance (2); Democratic primary candidates (2); Republican primary
 candidates (2); vote for David Duke as an independent (1); term limits
 (1); satisfaction with certain aspects of American life (10); role of
 the U.S. in the world (2); Persian Gulf War (1); vote for or against a
 congressman who took specific positions on certain issues (23).
