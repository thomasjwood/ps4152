1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Employee Benefit Research Institute
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, October, 1991 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of 1000.
  
Approximate number of variables: 28
Topical Coverage: 
 Government role in child care assistance (6); employer role in child
 care assistance (3); family leave (2).
