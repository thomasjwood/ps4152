1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, August 6-7, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 930.
  
Approximate number of variables: 22
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (1); party preference (1); voting record (1); voting
 intentions (3); George Bush job performance (1); presidential
 candidates (2); Persian Gulf (1); Bosnia (7).
