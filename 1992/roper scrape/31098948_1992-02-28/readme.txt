1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Washington Post, February 28-March 3, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1336.
  
Approximate number of variables: 27
Topical Coverage: 
 Race relations (6); racial attitudes (10); media role on racial
 relations (2); party identification (1).
