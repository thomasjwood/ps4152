1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Roper Organization, October 17-24, 1992 and based on
 face to face interviews with a national adult sample of 1996. Forms are
 located in column 5.  Combine punch 1 and punch 2 for form X.  Form Y =
 punch 3 and punch 4..
  
Approximate number of variables: 58
Topical Coverage: 
 National economic situation (1); voting intentions (5); candidates (1);
 currency devaluation (4); foreign corporations (2); NAFTA (3);
 evaluation of companies (3); Social Security (2); solid waste (5);
 shopping habits (3); eating habits (4); ideology (1); party preference
 (1).
