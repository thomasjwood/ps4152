1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, August 21, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 944.
  
Approximate number of variables: 48
Topical Coverage: 
 1992 presidential election (2); George Bush job performance (1); 1992
 presidential candidates (10); family values (5); candidates' wives (5);
 Republican convention (9); Bush's acceptance speech (2); Iraq (2).
