1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: ABC News / The Washington Post
 Conducted by ICR Survey Research Group, September 9-13, 1992 and based
 on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1002.
  
Approximate number of variables: 59
Topical Coverage: 
 Intention to vote (3); 1992 presidential trial heat (5); media coverage
 of presidential campaign (7); Harry Truman and the 1992 election (6).
