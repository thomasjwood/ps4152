1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: USA Today
 Conducted by Gordon S. Black Corporation, January 7-11, 1993 and based
 on telephone interviews with 809 General Managers and Sales Managers of
 automobile dealerships.
  
Approximate number of variables: 31
Topical Coverage: 
 Dealership record (4); dealership performance (8); evaluation of cars
 (9); evaluation of car producers (5); evaluation of car buyers (3).
