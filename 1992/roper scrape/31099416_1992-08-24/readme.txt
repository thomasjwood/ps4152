1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Nightly Business Report News Network & Reuters News Service
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, August 24-September 9, 1992
 and based on telephone interviews with 372 executives of American
 companies with more than $1 million in sales or revenue.
  
Approximate number of variables: 48
Topical Coverage: 
 North America Free Trade Agreement--NAFTA (21); 1992 presidential
 election (14).
