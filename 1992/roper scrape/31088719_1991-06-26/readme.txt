1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, June 26-29, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1000.
  
Approximate number of variables: 84
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); problems facing the country (2); South
 Africa (4); Iranian arms deal (1); John Sununu (1); considerations for
 choosing Democratic presidential nominee (10); political Presidential
 candidate positions (10); rating health care (6); rating medical care
 (5); cost of medical care (5); rating personal health care (8); health
 care reform (2).
