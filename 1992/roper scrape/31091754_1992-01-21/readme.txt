1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 January 21-27, 1992 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews
 with 500 adult residents of Connecticut.
  
Approximate number of variables: 72
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (7); attention paid to 1992 presidential
 candidates (2); impressions of various politicians (11); Senator
 Christopher Dodd job performance (1); 1992 Senatorial race (3);
 Governor Lowell Weicker job performance (1); imported vs. domestically
 manufactured goods (4); American made vs. foreign made cars (3); causes
 of trade imbalance with Japan (5); Japanese fairness in international
 trade policies (2); characteristics by which political candidates are
 judged (7); ethics, personal behavior and political office (8); smoking
 (6); dangers of smoking (2); rights of smokers (2); legislation and
 smoking (6).
