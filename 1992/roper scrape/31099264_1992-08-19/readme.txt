1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, August 19-20, 1992 and based
 on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1250.
  
Approximate number of variables: 64
Topical Coverage: 
 Party preference (1); national situation (1); voter registration (1);
 voting intentions (4); presidential candidates (8); George Bush job
 performance (2); opinion of political leaders (2); voting record (1);
 most important issue (1); political parties (3); Persian Gulf War (5);
 abortion (1); taxes (2); family values and moral issues (12); life
 satisfaction (1); Bosnia conflict (4).
