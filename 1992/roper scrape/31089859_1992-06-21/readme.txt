1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: WSB-TV
 Conducted by The Atlanta Journal-Constitution, June 21-24, 1992 and
 based on telephone interviews with 810 adult residents of twelve southern
 states.
  
Approximate number of variables: 37
Topical Coverage: 
 National situation (1); George Bush job performance (1); presidential
 candidates (2); 1992 presidential election (3); Dan Quayle job
 performance (1); government attention to what average people think
 (1);two-party system (1); political insiders and outsiders (1); 1992
 summer Olympics (11).
