1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, January 15-16, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 612.
  
Approximate number of variables: 51
Topical Coverage: 
 Motivation to succeed (5); self-esteem (11); factors in self-worth
 (12).
