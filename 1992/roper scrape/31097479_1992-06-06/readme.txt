1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Roper Organization, June 6-13,1992 and based on face
 to face interviews with a national adult sample of 1975.
  
Approximate number of variables: 247
Topical Coverage: 
 Performance of businesses (19); gender and public office (15); symbols
 of achievement (40); product quality by country (14); qualities of a
 good manager (12); sources of information (12); rating the boss (12).
