1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Washington Post, May 18-24, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with 505 teenagers--ages 12-17 years old.
  
Approximate number of variables: 48
Topical Coverage: 
 Satisfaction and confidence in the government (2); problems facing the
 country (1); trust in the government and leaders to do what is right
 (2); interest in politics (3); sources of political information (8).
