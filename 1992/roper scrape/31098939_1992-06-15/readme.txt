1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Washington Post, June 15-17, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with 1009 adult residents of Washington, DC metropolitan area.
  
Approximate number of variables: 34
Topical Coverage: 
 Weekend leisure activities (15); crime issues (2); new Camden Yards
 Stadium (3); Stadium (3); party preference (2).
