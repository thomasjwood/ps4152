1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Star Tribune, March 27- April 3, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with an adult residents of Minnesota sample of 830.
  
Approximate number of variables: 34
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (1); voting record (2); political interest (1);
 voting intentions (10); national situation (1); George Bush job
 performance (1); media coverage of campaign (1); personal economic
 situation (2); political cynicism (1); welfare abuse (1); minority (2);
 party preference (2); ideology (1).
