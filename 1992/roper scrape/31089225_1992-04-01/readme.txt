1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Employee Benefit Research Institute
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, April, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with a National adult--18-65 years old sample of 1000.
  
Approximate number of variables: 28
Topical Coverage: 
 Retirement age (4); retirement savings (11).
