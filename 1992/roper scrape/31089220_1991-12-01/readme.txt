1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Employee Benefit Research Institute
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, December, 1991 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of 1000.
  
Approximate number of variables: 27
Topical Coverage: 
 Satisfaction with health care coverage (1); relationship with family
 doctor (4); management and responsibilities for health care (6).
