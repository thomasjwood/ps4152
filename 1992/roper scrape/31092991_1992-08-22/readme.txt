1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Los Angeles Times, August 22-23, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with 1359 adult residents of Orange County.
  
Approximate number of variables: 48
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (1); voting intention (7); Proposition 165 (2);
 political leaders (8); presidential candidates (14); abortion (1);
 state budget (5); George Bush job performance (1); economic situation
 (3); political ideology (1).
