1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Des Moines Register, June 14-18, 22-23, 1992 and based
 on telephone (landline only) interviews with 265 likely voters in
 Congressional District 3, Iowa.
  
Approximate number of variables: 15
Topical Coverage: 
 Congressional candidate preferences (1); abortion (1); presidential
 campaign interest (1); overdrafts (1).
