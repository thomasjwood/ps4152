1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 October 15-21, 1991 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews
 with 500 adult residents of Connecticut. There are two
 versions of the questionnaire: 1) Asked only on October 15, 1991 and 2)
 Asked October 16-21, 1991.
  
Approximate number of variables: 92
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (5); Clarence Thomas (16); Connecticut
 Senators job performance (2); Lowell Weicker job performance (1); state
 legislature job performance (1); state taxes/budget (17); sexual
 harassment of women in workplace (14); national economy (2); personal
 financial situation (11).
