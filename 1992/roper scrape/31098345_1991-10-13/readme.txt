1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: USA Today
 Conducted by Gordon S. Black Corporation, October 13, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with 758 national adults including an oversample of
 African Americans.

Topical Coverage: 
 Clarence Thomas Supreme Court nomination hearings (8).
