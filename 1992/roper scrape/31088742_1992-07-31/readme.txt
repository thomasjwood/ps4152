1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, July 31-August 2, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1001.
  
Approximate number of variables: 26
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration(1); party preference(2); political ideology(1);
 voting intentions(4); George Bush job performance(1); national
 situation(1); Persian Gulf(3); Bosnia(2); women (4).
