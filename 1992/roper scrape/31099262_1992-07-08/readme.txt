1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, July 8-9, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1424.
  
Approximate number of variables: 67
Topical Coverage: 
 Party preference (1); political situation (4); voter registration (1);
 voting intentions (5); political candidates (24); George Bush job
 performance (1); opinion of political leaders (2); political parties
 (4); economic situation (7); Supreme Court (2); foreign affairs (5);
 newspaper readership (2).
