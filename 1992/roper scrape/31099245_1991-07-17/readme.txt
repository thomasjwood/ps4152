1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, July 17-18, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1000.
  
Approximate number of variables: 70
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); abortion (5); Supreme Court (12); AIDS
 (7).
