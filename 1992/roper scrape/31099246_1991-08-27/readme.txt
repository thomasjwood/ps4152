1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, August 27-18, 1991 and based
 on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1000.
  
Approximate number of variables: 65
Topical Coverage: 
 National situation (1); biggest problem facing the country (1); George
 Bush job performance (11); 1992 presidential candidates (3); economic
 situation (3); United States-Soviet relations (5); health care (32);
 American MIAs in southeast Asia (4); physical abuse of women (9);
 summer vacation (3); suicide (1); school health clinics and birth
 control (3); Elvis Presley (1).
