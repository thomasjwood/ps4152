1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: ABC News / The Washington Post
 Conducted by Chilton Research Services, October 24-27, 1991 and based
 on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1009.
  
Approximate number of variables: 87
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); confidence in American institutions
 (12); federal government/taxes (5); money problems/taxes (4); Dan
 Quayle job performance (5); political parties (20); presidential
 candidates (6).
