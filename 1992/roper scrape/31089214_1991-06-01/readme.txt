1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Employee Benefit Research Institute
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, June, 1991 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of 1001.
  
Approximate number of variables: 16
Topical Coverage: 
 Health care costs (6); health care insurance (2).
