1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Roper Organization, January 11-25, 1992 and based on
 face to face interviews with a national adult sample of 1978. There are
 2 forms for this study located in column 5.  Form X=1; Form Y=2.
  
Approximate number of variables: 56
Topical Coverage: 
 National economic situation (1); political interest (1); most important
 problems (5); general satisfaction (1); influential groups (1);
 personal concerns (1); cost of living (5); millionaires (4); evaluation
 of companies (3); special offers (3); shopping habits (3); physical
 exercise habits (5); taxes (3); workmates (1); evaluation of major
 cities (2).
