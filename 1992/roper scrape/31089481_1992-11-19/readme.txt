1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, November 19-20, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 764.
  
Approximate number of variables: 21
Topical Coverage: 
 Party preference (1); voting record (2); economic situation (3);
 evaluation of Bill Clinton transition (3); political leaders (1);
 Clinton administration (6).
