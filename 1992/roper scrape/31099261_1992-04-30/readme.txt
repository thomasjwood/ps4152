1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, April 30, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with 1102 national adults including an oversample of
 102 Blacks.
  
Approximate number of variables: 49
Topical Coverage: 
 Verdict in the Rodney King case (5); racial bias in the criminal
 justice system (2); police use of force (4); violence in Los Angeles
 after the verdict (5).
