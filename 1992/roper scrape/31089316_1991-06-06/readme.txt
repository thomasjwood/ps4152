1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: American Society for Quality Control
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, June 6-16, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1008. There are 2
 forms for this study located in column 22.  Form 1=1; Form 2=2; Form
 3=3; Form 4=4.
  
Approximate number of variables: 80
Topical Coverage: 
 Determination of product quality (1); influences on purchase decision
 (11); willingness to pay for quality (4); determination of service
 quality (1); rating the quality of certain service industries (7);
 rating the quality of American/Japanese/German products (3); effort of
 business to deliver quality (4); importance of quality to American/
 Japanese/German workers (3); Japanese/German product purchases (7);
 foreign product purchases (1); rating product quality by country of
 origin (7).
