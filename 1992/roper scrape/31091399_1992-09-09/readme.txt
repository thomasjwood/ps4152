1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by CBS News and The New York Times, September 9-13, 1992 and
 based on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1329.
  
Approximate number of variables: 93
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (5); voting intention (9); voter
 registration (2); economic situation (6); voting record (4); party
 preference (2); opinion of political leaders (5); support for
 candidates (3); political interest (2); presidential candidates (28);
 political knowledge (2); family values (1); hurricane Andrew (2);
 environment (2).
