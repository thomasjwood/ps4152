1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Roper Organization, July 6-13, 1991 and based on face
 to face interviews with a national adult sample of 1981.
  
Approximate number of variables: 175
Topical Coverage: 
 Economic situation (3); interest in environmental issues (2); personal
 budget planning (1); lobbyists (3); South Africa (5); retirement (26);
 cable television (2); satisfaction with living area (2); American
 business and industry issues (6); life-experience and life-style (5);
 weddings (5); ideology (1); party identification (1); political
 participation (2).
