1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Star Tribune, July 7-14, 1991 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with 600 adult residents of Minnesota.
  
Approximate number of variables: 30
Topical Coverage: 
 Arne Carlson job performance (10); Dave Durenberger job performance
 (1); Paul Wellstone job performance (1); Persian Gulf (4); cancer (1);
 AIDS (13).
