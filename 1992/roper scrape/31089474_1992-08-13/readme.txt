1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, August 13-14, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 946.
  
Approximate number of variables: 38
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush vs. Bill Clinton (2); reasons not to vote for George Bush
 (14); George Bush job performance (1); James Baker as White House Chief
 of Staff (1); George Bush's policies affecting certain areas (11);
 George Bush handling former Yugoslav Republic of Bosnia (1); U.S.
 handling Serbian blocks of relief to Bosnia (1); 1973 Roe vs. Wade (1);
 George Bush's opinions on abortion (2); abortion (1); George Bush
 having affair when vice-president (2); Bill Clinton's marriage
 infidelity (1).
