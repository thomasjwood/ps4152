1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: USA Today
 Conducted by Gordon S. Black Corporation, February 25-27, 1992 and
 based on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 604.
  
Approximate number of variables: 40
Topical Coverage: 
 U.S.-Japanese trade imbalance (1); better for Americans to buy only
 U.S. product s(2); importance of "American-made" in purchase of car and
 clothing (2); knowledge of where certain companies are based (7);
 knowledge of where certain products are made (5); efforts to buy
 American (1); determination of an American made product (1); frequency
 of checking the label of clothing (2); choice of foreign product
 instead of American because of price, quality or availability in the
 last three months (1); plant closings (2).
