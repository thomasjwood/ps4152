1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Des Moines Register, December 2-5, 7-8, 1991 and based
 on telephone (landline only) interviews with 806 adult residents of Iowa.
  
Approximate number of variables: 56
Topical Coverage: 
 Politician job approvals (4); precinct caucuses (1); presidential
 candidate preferences (9); voter participation (1); senator candidate
 preferences (1); party affiliation (1); George Bush loyalty (1);
 economy direction (2); financial situation (1); health insurance (5);
 insurance adequacy (1); medical bills (2); national healthcare (2);
 Equal Rights Amendment (1); AIDS prevention (4); shopping money (1);
 racial diversity (2); sexual harassment (3); busyness (3).
