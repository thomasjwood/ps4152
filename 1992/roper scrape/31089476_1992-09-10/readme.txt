1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, September 10-11, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 920.
  
Approximate number of variables: 24
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (1); party preference (2); voting intentions (2);
 George Bush job performance (2); presidential candidates (4); economic
 situation (2); federal budget deficit (3).
