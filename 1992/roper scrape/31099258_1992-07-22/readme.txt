1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, July 22-23, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1050.
  
Approximate number of variables: 52
Topical Coverage: 
 Party preference (1); voter registration (1); voting intentions (5);
 opinion of political leaders (3); George Bush job performance (1);
 presidential candidates (7); political parties (3); the next millenium
 (11); religion (3); fashion (2); world situation (2); most admired
 person (3).
