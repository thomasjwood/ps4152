1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, March 11-12, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1400.
  
Approximate number of variables: 63
Topical Coverage: 
 National situation (1); most important issue (1); voter registration
 (1); party preference (2); voting intention (5); George Bush job
 performance (2); presidential candidates (9); role of U.S. in world
 affairs (1); political leaders (5); economic situation (6); welfare
 system (6); nuclear power (1); children (3); assessment of the
 political ideology of the media (1).
