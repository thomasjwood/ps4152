1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Los Angeles Times, December 4-8, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with 1128 adult residents of Orange County including
 an oversample of adults 60 years old and over.
  
Approximate number of variables: 142
Topical Coverage: 
 Life satisfaction (2); personal problems (2); satisfaction with home
 (1); troops in Somalia (3); health (1); personal finances (1);
 affordable medical care (1); government medical coverage (2); private
 medical insurance (2); importance of religion (1); socializing (3); age
 of friends (2); quality of life for elderly (2); problems for elder vs.
 young (1); children supporting parents (1); parents living with grown
 children (1); live to 100 (1); life sustaining medical treatment (1);
 guaranteed health care coverage (1); elderly drivers renewing license
 (1); contact with family (7); age of parents (1); self-sufficient
 parents (3); assistance to parents (10); self-sufficiency (1); living
 arrangement (1); needing assistance (1); being a senior citizen (3);
 planning (2); transportation (1); finances for retirement (14); elderly
 program (1); age discrimination (2); worry about life as elderly (1);
 quality of life as elderly (1); support from Social Security (1);
 living conditions for retired persons (1); retirement leads to early
 death (1); encouraging early retirement (1); long-term nursing care for
 elderly (1); activities last week (1); feelings on retirement (1);
 preparation for retirement (1); earning money after retirement (1);
 activities after retirement (1); fears in old age (2); care in nursing
 home (1); loneliness (1); health problems (1); death (2); suicide (1);
 living situation (2); children living at home (3).
