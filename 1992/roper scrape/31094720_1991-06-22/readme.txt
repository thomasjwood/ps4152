1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: NBC News and The Wall Street Journal
 Conducted by Hart-Teeter Research Companies, June 22-25, 1991 and based
 on telephone interviews with a Registered voters sample of 1006.
  
Approximate number of variables: 93
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (3); impressions of political leaders (6);
 John Sunnunu (2); economy (9); personal financial situation (6);
 foreign aid (6); U.S.-Soviet relations (13); civil rights/affirmative
 action (14); health care (10).
