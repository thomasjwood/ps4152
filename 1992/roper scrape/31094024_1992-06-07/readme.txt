1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Star Tribune, June 7-14, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with 1003 adult residents of Minnesota.
  
Approximate number of variables: 78
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (1); voting record (2); voting intention (8);
 political interest (2); state political leaders (3); media coverage of
 campaign (2); candidates (23); George Bush job performance (2);
 evaluation of political system (8); welfare abuse (1); public education
 (1); minority (2); personal economic situation (2); national economic
 situation (3); Mall of America (4); party preference (2); ideology (1);
 abortion (2).
