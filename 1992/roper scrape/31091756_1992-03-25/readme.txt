1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 March 25-30, 1992 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews
 with 500 adult residents of Connecticut.
  
Approximate number of variables: 72
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (2); 1992 presidential candidates (4);
 impressions of various politicians (10); potential winners of party
 nominations (2); candidates for 1992 election (7); character and
 integrity of candidates  (3); Senator Christopher Dodd job performance
 (2); 1992 candidates for Senate (2); Governor Lowell Weicker job
 performance (1); General Assembly job performance (1); job performance
 of US government (2); check bouncing scandal (9); gambling (4); effect
 of Hartford casino on Connecticut (8); transportation (11).
