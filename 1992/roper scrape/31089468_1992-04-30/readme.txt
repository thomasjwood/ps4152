1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, April 30-May 1, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a National adult including an oversample of
 205 Black adults sample of 608.
  
Approximate number of variables: 21
Topical Coverage: 
 Vote in Presidential three-way election (2); George Bush job
 performance (2); race relations (3); police (2); Rodney King verdict
 (2); riot in Los Angeles (1); rating of police handling of the riots
 (1).
