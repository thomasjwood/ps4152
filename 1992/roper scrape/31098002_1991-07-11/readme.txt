1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Times Mirror Center for the People & the Press
 Conducted by Princeton Survey Research Associates, July 11-14, 1991 and
 based on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1212.
  
Approximate number of variables: 
Topical Coverage: 
 Attention to news events(14); stories receiving too much coverage(9);
 press job performance(2); bias of news organization(8).
