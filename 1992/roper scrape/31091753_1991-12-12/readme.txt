1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 December 12-17, 1991 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews
 with a Adult residents of Connecticut sample of 500.
  
Approximate number of variables: 63
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (2); U.S. economy (1); family financial
 situation (2); Governor Lowell Weicker job performance (1); General
 Assembly performance (1); level of state taxes (1); level of state
 spending (9); role of state government (12); quality of state services
 (2); personal knowledge of some one working for the state (2); causes
 of higher state spending (5); personal benefits of state services (5);
 impressions of cuts in various programs (7); cuts in state spending
 (9).
