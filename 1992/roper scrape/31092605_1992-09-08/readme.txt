1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Des Moines Register, September 8-10, 12-15, 1992 and
 based on telephone (landline only) interviews with 814 likely voters in
 Iowa.
  
Approximate number of variables: 111
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter participation (1); politician job performances (9); presidential
 candidate preferences (3); senator candidate preferences (1); county
 (1); congressional candidate preferences (1); George Bush statements
 (4); Bill Clinton statements (4); important candidate aspects (15);
 right direction (1); confidence in government (2); agreement with
 various statements (6); Equal Rights Amendment (2); events affecting
 election (9); party affiliation (4); lifestyle changes as problem
 causes (11); Ronald Reagan job performance (1); Jimmy Carter job
 performance (1); media approval (1); question for candidates (1);
 economy direction (3); community ratings (7); financial outlook (2);
 politician favorability (5); Ross Perot campaign (1); abortion (1).
