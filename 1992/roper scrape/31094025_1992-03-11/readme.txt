1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Star Tribune, March 11-18, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with 917 adult residents of Minnesota.
  
Approximate number of variables: 58
Topical Coverage: 
 Voting intention (18); voter registration (1); voting record (2);
 political interest (1); national situation (1); candidates (16); George
 Bush job performance (20); media coverage of campaign (1); personal
 economic situation (2); political cynicism (10); welfare abuse (1);
 minorities (2); party preference (2); ideology (1).
