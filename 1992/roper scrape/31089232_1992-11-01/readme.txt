1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Employee Benefit Research Institute
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, November, 1992 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of 1000.
  
Approximate number of variables: 23
Topical Coverage: 
 Taxation of employee health benefits (12).
