1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, February 6-9, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1002.
  
Approximate number of variables: 33
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (2); presidential candidates (5); political
 parties (2); Congressional job performance (1); financial situation
 (2); foreign competition (9); immigration (9); Persian Gulf War (3).
