1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Times Mirror Center for the People & the Press
 Conducted by Princeton Survey Research Associates, October 3-6, 1991
 and based on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1211.
  
Approximate number of variables: 82
Topical Coverage: 
 Attention to news events (13); stories receiving too much coverage
 (11); press job performance (2); relations with Soviet Union (5); 1992
 presidential election (11); presidential candidates and the media (10).
