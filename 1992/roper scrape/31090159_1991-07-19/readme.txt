1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Associated Press
 Conducted by ICR Survey Research Group, July 19-23, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1007.
  
Approximate number of variables: 32
Topical Coverage: 
 Impression of Soviet Union (1); Soviet reform (2); U.S. foreign aid to
 the Soviet Union (2).
