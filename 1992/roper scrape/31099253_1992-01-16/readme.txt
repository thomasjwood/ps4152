1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, January 16, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1000.
  
Approximate number of variables: 54
Topical Coverage: 
 National situation (1); 1992 presidential election (3); 1992
 presidential candidates (24); George Bush job performance (2); Bush's
 trip to Japan (3); environment (3); Soviet Union (3); economy (6).
