1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Roper Organization, March 7-14, 1992 and based on face
 to face interviews with a national adult sample of 1994. There are 2
 forms for this study located in column 5.  Form X=1; Form Y=2.
  
Approximate number of variables: 267
Topical Coverage: 
 Personal financial situation (1); values of government services (2);
 immigrants (2); affirmative action (1); conditions for blacks (1);
 credit cards (5); food labels (7); food safety (7); sports and hobbies
 (2); time travel (2).
