1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Partners, Inc., January 13-14, 1993 and based
 on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1000.
  
Approximate number of variables: 92
Topical Coverage: 
 Confidence in Bill Clinton (15); expectations of Bill Clinton (5);
 personal financial situation (3); economic situation (3); taxes (10);
 George Bush (4); Iraq (5); Clinton cabinet (5); Clinton style of
 leadership (6); U.S. troops in Somalia (4); Bosnia (3).
