1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Center for Public Policy, Brown University, June 10-12,
 1991 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews with 437
 registered voters in Rhode Island.
  
Approximate number of variables: 30
Topical Coverage: 
 Rhode Island economy (2); personal financial situation (2); George Bush
 job performance (1); Dan Quayle job performance (1); John Chafee job
 performance (1); Claiborne Pell job performance (1); Sundlum job
 performance (2); confidence in Sundlum reducing budget deficit (1);
 change in number of school districts (3); affects of reducing number of
 districts (3); betting on out-of-state dog/horse racing (1); income tax
 reform (1); abortion (1); redistricting (1); state pension system (3).
