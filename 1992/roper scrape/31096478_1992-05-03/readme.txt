1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: U.S. News and World Report
 Conducted by Princeton Survey Research Associates, May 3-7, 1992 and
 based on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1200.
 There are 2 forms for this study located in column 86.  Form 1=1; Form
 2=2.
  
Approximate number of variables: 175
Topical Coverage: 
 Presidential candidates (3); presidential vote intention (1);
 satisfaction with the way things are going in the U.S. (1); biggest
 problem facing the family (1); personal financial situation (3);
 employment situation and expectations (9); business conditions in the
 community (3); ability to afford certain things (12); effects of
 various economic policy measures (10); willingness to pay taxes to
 accomplish certain national goals (4); presidential candidate best able
 to handle certain policy areas (12).
