1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by CBS News, January 14-19, 1992 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of 2897.
  
Approximate number of variables: 42
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (3); voting intention (2); voter
 registration (1) economic situation (7); voting record (1); party
 preference (2); political interest (1); taxing and spending (6); class
 status (1); state of the union address (2); candidates (2).
