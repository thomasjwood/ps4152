1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by CBS News, October 8-9, 2001 and based on telephone
 interviews with a national adult sample of 870.
  
Approximate number of variables: 100
Topical Coverage: 
 George W. Bush Job Performance (2); Military Action (4); Humanitarian
 Aid (1); Osama Bin Laden (2); Alliances (1); Casualties (2); Terrorism
 (4); Safety of Country (2); Fear (1); Arabs (2); Disagreeing with
 President (2).
