1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Times Mirror Center
 Conducted by Princeton Survey Research Associates, October 31-November
 10, 1991 and based on telephone interviews with a national adult sample
 of 2020.
  
Approximate number of variables: 119
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); 1992 presidential election (8); most
 important problem facing the respondent (1); satisfied with the
 direction of the country (1); most important priority for the next
 president (2); economy (1); changes to the political system (1);
 personal financial situation (3); employment (2); types of candidates
 running for office (6); agree/disagree with political statements (14);
 situation that has had the greatest effect on the respondent's
 political beliefs (2); spending on defense (1); agree/disagree with
 certain statements (48); political leaders/organizations job
 performance (16); frequency of voting (1).
