1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by New York Times, October 24-26, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with 1559 adult residents of New York State.
  
Approximate number of variables: 68
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (1); opinion of political leaders (7); voting
 intention (13); political interest (2); most important issue (1);
 presidential candidates (9); presidential campaign (8); abortion (1);
 health care (1); gays in military (1); death penalty (1); government
 spending (1); economic situation (2); voting record (6); party
 preference (2); ideology (1).
