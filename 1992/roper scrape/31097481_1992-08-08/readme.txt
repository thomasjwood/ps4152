1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Roper Organization, August 8-15, 1992 and based on
 face to face interviews with a national adult sample of 2000. Forms are
 located in column 5.  Combine punch 1 and punch 2 for form X.  Form Y =
 3.
  
Approximate number of variables: 78
Topical Coverage: 
 National economic situation (1); national situation (6); Bill Clinton
 job performance (10); evaluation of political and social leaders (10);
 divided government (1); personal economic situation (8); labor unions
 (6); evaluation of federal government agencies (2); communications (3);
 reading habits (3); non-profit organizations (4); ideology (1); party
 preference (1).
