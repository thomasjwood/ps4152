1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 August 27-September 2, 1992 and based on telephone (landline only)
 interviews with 869 adult residents of Connecticut.
  
Approximate number of variables: 75
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (3); party preference (1); political
 ideology (1); presidential candidates (18); voting intentions (5);
 state political leaders (12); economic situation (1); family values
 (2); Persian Gulf (10); Bosnia (2); personal concerns (5); crime (5);
 union membership (1); voter registration (2).
