1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 October 23-30, 1992 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews
 with 1322 adult residents of Connecticut.
  
Approximate number of variables: 41
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); party preference (1); political
 ideology (1); presidential candidates (6); voting intentions (10);
 state political leaders (8); economic situation (3); national situation
 (3); voting record (2).
