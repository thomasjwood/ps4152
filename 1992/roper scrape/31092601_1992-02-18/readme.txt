1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Des Moines Register, February 18-20, 24-25, 1992 and
 based on telephone (landline only) interviews with 810 adult residents of
 Iowa.
  
Approximate number of variables: 50
Topical Coverage: 
 Politician job performances (4); most important election issue (1);
 legislature confidence (4); Joseph Welsh controversy (2); statehouse
 ethical problems (3); ethics proposals (5); handling ethics problems
 (1); labor force (2); worker writing skills (1); prison construction
 (1); paying for prison construction (1); environmental protection (6);
 environmental problems solutions (1); low interest rates (5); Kennedy
 assassination (3).
