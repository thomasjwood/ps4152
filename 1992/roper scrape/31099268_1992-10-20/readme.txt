1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, October 20-22, 1992 and based
 on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1653.
  
Approximate number of variables: 60
Topical Coverage: 
 Party preference (1); national situation (1); voter registration (1);
 voting intentions (6); presidential candidates (17); George Bush job
 performance (2); opinion of political leaders (4); voting record (1);
 presidential debates (4); political parties (1); policy preferences
 (3); economy (2); holidays (4).
