1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Des Moines Register, June 3-11, 1991 and based on
 telephone (landline only) interviews with 808 adult residents of Iowa.
  
Approximate number of variables: 92
Topical Coverage: 
 Senator Tom Harkin job performance (1); George Bush job performance
 (3); Senator Charles Grassley job performance (1); Governor Terry
 Branstad job performance (1); Persian Gulf War (1); November state
 elections (3); abortion (3); nutrition (26).
