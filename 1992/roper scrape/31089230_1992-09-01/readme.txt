1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Employee Benefit Research Institute
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, September 1992 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of 1000.
  
Approximate number of variables: 57
Topical Coverage: 
 Job benefit importance (3); health insurance provider (1); health
 insurance cost (1); employer contribution to health insurance costs
 (2); pension (2); importance of specific benefits (36); jobs with lack
 of benefits (2).
