1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, October 11, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a Panel of registered voters who volunteered
 to be called immediately following the October 11th presidential
 debate. sample of 433.
  
Approximate number of variables: 61
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush vs. Bill Clinton vs. Ross Perot in debates (1); debate
 swaying respondent's opinions on certain issues (3); statements
 applying to members of the debate (6); best answers to certain issues
 (5); rating panel of journalists (1); seriousness of Ross Perot (1);
 George Bush and Dan Quayle vs. Bill Clinton and Al Gore vs. Ross Perot
 and James Stockdale (2); switching vote from Ross Perot if he is
 loosing (1); important issues that sway vote (10); candidates handling
 of issues (9); creating jobs and improving economy (1); North American
 Free Trade Agreement--NAFTA (1); television advertising George Bush
 (5); television advertising Bill Clinton (5); television advertising
 Ross Perot (5); satisfaction with Bill Clinton's anti-Vietnam and
 Soviet Union activities (4).
