1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: ABC News / The Washington Post
 Conducted by ICR Survey Research Group, September 23-27, 1992 and based
 on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1005.
  
Approximate number of variables: 54
Topical Coverage: 
 Intention to vote (3); 1992 presidential trial heat (9); impressions of
 presidential candidates (5).
