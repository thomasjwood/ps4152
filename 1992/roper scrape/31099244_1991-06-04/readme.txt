1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, June 4-5, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1000.
  
Approximate number of variables: 45
Topical Coverage: 
 National situation (1); George Bush job performance (10); biggest
 problem facing the country (1); Persian Gulf War (17); organ
 transplants (8); televised broadcasts of executions (2); quality of
 television programs (5); sexual morality (7).
