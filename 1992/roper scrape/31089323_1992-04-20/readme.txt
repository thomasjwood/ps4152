1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Independent Sector
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, April-May 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with 1404 teens--12-17 years old.
  
Approximate number of variables: 677
Topical Coverage: 
 Importance of various life goals (15); confidence in various charities
 (13); confidence in various institutions (14); organizations
 volunteered for (92); hours spent volunteering (75); extra curricular
 volunteer activities (15); way of getting into volunteering (15); age
 of first volunteering (1); first learning about volunteer opportunities
 (3); reasons for volunteering (21); time spent volunteering three years
 ago (1); importance of volunteer benefits (18); past volunteering
 experience (1); reason for stopping volunteering (1); rating volunteer
 experience (1); reasons to start volunteering again (1); being asked to
 volunteer (1); contributing money to charities (27); school encouraging
 community service (1); required service hours for graduation (2);
 offering course credit for community service (3); helping needy people
 directly (5); active church members in family (1); feelings toward
 required service (1); grade point average (1); religious ideology (1);
 support for social issues (3); agreement with personal view statements
 (10); activities occurred when young (13); genuine concern for needy
 people (1); examples of caring people (2); counting on others for
 family illness help (7); activities done in past year (15); reasons for
 being kind and caring (9); satisfaction gained from various sources
 (6); attraction to various careers (12); ways to make society better
 (10); parent volunteer work (1); people in life showing self kindness
 (2); imagined future activities (12); activities actually completed
 (12); rating feelings of various accomplishments (9); societal efforts
 for various causes (7); importance of various values (10).
