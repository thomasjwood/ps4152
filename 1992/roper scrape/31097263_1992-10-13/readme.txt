1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Center for Public Policy, Brown University, October 13-15,
 1992 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews with 402
 registered voters in Rhode Island.
  
Approximate number of variables: 42
Topical Coverage: 
 Interest in public affairs (1); attention to state legislature (1);
 General Assembly job performance (9); General Assembly greatest
 achievement/greatest failure (2); personal contact with legislators
 (7); legislature interest in the public interest (2); type of
 legislature (1); changes within legislature (8); public initiative
 policy (1); new legislature (2).
