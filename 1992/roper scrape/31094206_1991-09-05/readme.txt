1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Council on Competitiveness
 Conducted by Peter D. Hart Research Associates and American Viewpoint,
 September 5-13, 1991 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews
 with a Registered voters sample of 1003. There are 2 forms for this
 study located in column 12.  Form A=1; Form B=2.
  
Approximate number of variables: 67
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (1); national situation (1); most important problems
 (6); national economic situation (7); personal economic situation (3);
 U.S. economic competitiveness (25); national economic priorities (2);
 influence of economic issues on candidate preference (3); economic role
 of government (3).
