1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Washington Post, August 27-30, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with 1244 adult residents of the District of Columbia and
 Virginia.
  
Approximate number of variables: 38
Topical Coverage: 
 Political leaders (1); Redskins football (15); new stadium (9); party
 preference (2); voter registration (1).
