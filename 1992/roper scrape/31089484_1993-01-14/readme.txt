1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, January 14-15, 1993 and based on
 telephone interviews with a National adults who claim to have voted in
 the 1992 presidential election sample of 753.
  
Approximate number of variables: 28
Topical Coverage: 
 Party preference (1); voting record (2); George Bush job performance
 (1); evaluation of Clinton administration (6); U.S. military presence
 overseas (7); policy preferences (3); evaluation of Hillary Clinton's
 role (2).
