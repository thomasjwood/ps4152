1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Des Moines Register, September 8-10, 12-15, 1992 and
 based on telephone (landline only) interviews with 259 likely voters in
 Congressional District 2, Iowa.
  
Approximate number of variables: 19
Topical Coverage: 
 Congressional candidate preferences (1); right direction (1);
 government confidence (1); events affecting election (3); party
 affiliation (1); abortion (1).
