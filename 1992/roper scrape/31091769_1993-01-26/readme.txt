1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 January 26-February 3, 1993 and based on telephone (landline only)
 interviews with an adult residents of Connecticut sample of 500.
  
Approximate number of variables: 87
Topical Coverage: 
 Bill Clinton job performance (2); federal budget deficit (3); Zoe Baird
 nomination for attorney general (7); gays in the military (4); Governor
 Lowell Weicker job performance (1); state spending and the state income
 tax (11); gambling (9); racial integration of public schools (24).
