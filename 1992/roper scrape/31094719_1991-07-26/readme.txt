1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: NBC News and The Wall Street Journal
 Conducted by Hart-Teeter Research Companies, July 26-29, 1991 and based
 on telephone interviews with 1004 registered voters.
  
Approximate number of variables: 71
Topical Coverage: 
 Bush job performance(4); role of the president(2); US right direction
 or wrong track(1); state of the economy(5); Congressional job
 performance(2); Congressional vote(1); divided government(1);
 Democratic primary election(4); 1992 election(2); Gates CIA
 nomination(2); MIAs and POWs(2); Clarence Thomas confirmation(3); aid
 to the Soviet Union(5); reduction of nuclear arsenals(5); Middle East,
 Israeli occupied territories(3); the Gulf war(2); the environment(13);
 US energy Policy(11).
