1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, August 8-23, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1019. There are 2
 forms for this study located in column 22.  Form 1=1; Form 2=2.
  
Approximate number of variables: 42
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); party preference (1).
