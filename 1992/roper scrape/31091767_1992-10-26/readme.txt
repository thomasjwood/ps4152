1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 October 26-29, 1992 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews
 with an adult residents of Willington, Connecticut sample of 279.
  
Approximate number of variables: 20
Topical Coverage: 
 Party preference (1); political ideology (1); voting intentions (11);
 evaluation of state officials (1); voting record (2).
