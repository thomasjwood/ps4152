1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: USA Today
 Conducted by Gordon S. Black Corporation, May 6-7, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1000.
  
Approximate number of variables: 17
Topical Coverage: 
 Satisfaction with political and economic conditions (1); environment
 vs. energy (2); energy alternation (1); likely developments in meeting
 energy needs in the future (1); seriousness global warming (1);
 knowledge and the Earth Summit (1); global warming treaty (2); U.S.
 finding of environmental technologies (1).
