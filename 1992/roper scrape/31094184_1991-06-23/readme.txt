1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Americans Talk Issues Foundation
 Conducted by Greenberg-Lake: The Analysis Group, June 23-July 1, 1991
 and based on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1002.
  
Approximate number of variables: 102
Topical Coverage: 
 Feelings towards various countries (8); feelings towards different
 groups (5); Persian Gulf war (21); new world order/United Nations (21);
 environmental problems (6); military intervention (1).
