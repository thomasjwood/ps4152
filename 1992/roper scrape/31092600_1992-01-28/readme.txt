1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Des Moines Register, January 28-30, February 1-6, 1992
 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews with 602 likely
 Democratic Caucus participants in Iowa.
  
Approximate number of variables: 29
Topical Coverage: 
 Precinct caucus attendance (1); presidential candidate preferences (2);
 Tom Harkin support reasons (6); Harkin campaign efforts (1); caucus
 satisfaction (2); Democratic nominee (1); candidate sexual conduct (3).
