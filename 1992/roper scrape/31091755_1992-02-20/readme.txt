1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 February 20-25, 1992 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews
 with 500 adult residents of Connecticut.
  
Approximate number of variables: 70
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (11); 1992 presidential campaigns (4);
 impressions of various politicians (8); Senator Christopher Dodd job
 performance (1); General Assembly job performance (1); level of states
 taxes and spending (2); impressions of various types of gambling (6);
 opinion of gambling (2); participation in gambling (2); visit to a
 casino (3); effect on gambling in state (3); compulsive gambling (3);
 supermarket tabloids (10); weather  (3); climate of Connecticut (4);
 greenhouse effect (2).
