1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: ABC News / The Washington Post
 Conducted by Chilton Research Services, October 15-20, 1991 and based
 on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1536.
  
Approximate number of variables: 83
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (6); economy (3); most important problems
 (6); political parties (2); Congress/government trust/politics (27);
 sick/paid leave (3); Soviet Union (3); cuts in military spending (2);
 Clarence Thomas (6).
