1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Times Mirror Center for the People & the Press
 Conducted by Princeton Survey Research Associates, October 20-22, 1992
 and based on telephone interviews with 1153 registered voter callback.
 Respondents were originally interviewed October 8-11,
 1992.  There are 2 forms for this study located in column 239.  Form
 1=1; Form 2=2.
  
Approximate number of variables: 88
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (1); voter interest, involvement (6); George Bush/
 Dan Quayle vs. Bill Clinton/Al Gore vs. Ross Perot/James Stockdale
 (11); George Bush (5); Bill Clinton (5); Ross Perot (7); Perot
 commercials (3); presidential debates (2); method of questioning in
 debates (1); candidates handling certain problems (29); describing
 candidates (30); candidate to win election (3); opinion of certain
 statements (17); satisfaction with candidates (1); highest priority for
 president (2); personal finances (1); economic conditions (1); free
 trade (1); Bush vs. Clinton (1); opinion of certain statements (8);
 following campaign news stories (3).
