1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Star Tribune, August 6-25, 1991 and based on telephone
 interviews with a national adult sample of 1101.
  
Approximate number of variables: 40
Topical Coverage: 
 Crime (8); death penalty (3); sex crimes (29).
