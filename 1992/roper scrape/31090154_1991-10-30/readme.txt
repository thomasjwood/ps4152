1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Associated Press
 Conducted by ICR Survey Research Group, October 30-November 3, 1991 and
 based on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1006.
  
Approximate number of variables: 5
Topical Coverage: 
 Term limitations for members of Congress (3); euthanasia (1); laws
 against discrimination against homosexuals (1).
