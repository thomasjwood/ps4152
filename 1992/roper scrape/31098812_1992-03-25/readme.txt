1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by WABC-TV, March 25-26, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with 736 adult residents of New York State and an oversample
 of New York City residents.
  
Approximate number of variables: 34
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); Congressional job performance (2);
 candidates issues (9); most important problem (1); state government
 performance (2); state economic situation (2); voting intentions (6);
 campaign issues (1); racial issues (2).
