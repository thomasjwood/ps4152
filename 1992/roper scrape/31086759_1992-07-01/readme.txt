1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: ABC News / The Washington Post
 Conducted by Chilton Research Services, July 1-8, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1506.
  
Approximate number of variables: 67
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (3); political interest (5); voting
 intentions (6); election issues (18); political parties (8); campaign
 issues (3); evaluation of government performance (1); party
 identification (2); national issues (6); ideology (1); voting record
 (2); voter registration (1).
