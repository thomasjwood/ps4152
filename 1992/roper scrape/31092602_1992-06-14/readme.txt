1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Des Moines Register, June 14-18 and 22-23, 1992 and
 based on telephone (landline only) interviews with 810 adult residents of
 Iowa.
  
Approximate number of variables: 77
Topical Coverage: 
 Politician job approvals (7); voter participation (1); presidential
 candidate preferences (4); county (1); congressional candidate
 preference (1); abortion (1); Dan Quayle as running mate (1); George
 Bush statements (4); Bill Clinton statements (4); Ross Perot statements
 (4); Perot favorability (2); interest in politics (3); attitude toward
 politics (4); term limits (1); overdrafts (1); Terry Branstad job
 performance (2); legalized gambling (7); snoring (3); favorite TV
 character (1); celebrities as neighbors (11); answering machines (3).
