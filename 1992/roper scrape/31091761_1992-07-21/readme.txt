1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 July 21-27, 1992 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews with
 832 adult residents of Connecticut plus an oversample of 132 registered
 voters.
  
Approximate number of variables: 74
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (2); party preference (1); political
 ideology (1); presidential candidates (19); voting intentions (5);
 state political leaders (12); economic situation (1); Persian Gulf
 (14); crime (9); union membership (1); voter registration (2).
