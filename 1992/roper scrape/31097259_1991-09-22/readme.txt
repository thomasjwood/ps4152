1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Center for Public Policy, Brown University, September 22-
 24, 1991 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews with 402
 registered voters in Rhode Island.
  
Approximate number of variables: 32
Topical Coverage: 
 Rhode Island economy (2); George Bush job performance (1); John Chafee
 job performance (1); Claiborne Pell job performance (1); Bruce Sundlun
 job performance (1); Rhode Island on the right track (1); personal
 funds in frozen accounts (1); government pay back of frozen accounts
 (2); blame for banking crisis (9); proposed Green airport construction
 (1); Rhode Island legal justice system (1); new judge probation period
 (1); mandatory ethics program for judges (1); judicial selection (2);
 Clarence Thomas (1).
