1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, April 9-10, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with 328 Super delegates to the 1992 Democratic
 National Convention.
  
Approximate number of variables: 16
Topical Coverage: 
 Vote for Bill Clinton if he still needs delegates (1); Clinton have the
 right skills to be President (2); can Clinton beat George Bush (1);
 support for Clinton (1); will Clinton help other Democratic candidates
 (1); Ross Perot (1).
