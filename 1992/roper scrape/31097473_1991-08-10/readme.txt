1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Roper Organization, August 10-17, 1991 and based on
 face to face interviews with a national adult sample of 1984.
  
Approximate number of variables: 177
Topical Coverage: 
 Federal departments and agencies (9); effects of big business on
 societal problems (12); investments (20); business responsibilities
 (1); employment (10); life styles (6); major purchases (3);
 advertisements (1); media/ magazines (7).
