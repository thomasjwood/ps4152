1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Washington Post, October 23-27, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1003.
  
Approximate number of variables: 63
Topical Coverage: 
 Concerns about national issues (15); attention paid to specific issues
 during campaign (15).
