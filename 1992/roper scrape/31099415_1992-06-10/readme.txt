1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Nightly Business Report News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, June 10-19, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with 364 executives of American companies with more
 than $1 million in sales or revenue.
  
Approximate number of variables: 42
Topical Coverage: 
 Health care system (3); economic stimulus (7); federal budget deficit
 and government spending (5); international trade (6); George Bush vs.
 Bill Clinton vs. Ross Perot on the issues (7).
