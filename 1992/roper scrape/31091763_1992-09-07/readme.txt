1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 September 7-11, 1992 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews
 with 543 adult residents of Connecticut who are eligible to vote in the
 Republican primary.
  
Approximate number of variables: 34
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); party preference (1); political
 ideology (1); presidential candidates (7); voting intentions (9); state
 political leaders performance (5); campaign issues (4); voting record
 (2). voting record (2).
