1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, July 17, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with a national adult sample of 955.
  
Approximate number of variables: 25
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (1); party preference (2); voting record (1); voting
 intention (5); presidential candidates (7); Democratic party (4).
