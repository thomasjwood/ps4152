1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: ABC News / The Washington Post
 Conducted by ICR Survey Research Group, August 7-11, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1009.

Topical Coverage: 
 Intention to vote (3); 1992 presidential trial heat (5).
