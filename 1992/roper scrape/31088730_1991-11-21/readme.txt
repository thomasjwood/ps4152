1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, November 21-24, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1005.
  
Approximate number of variables: 57
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); most important problem (3); Japan job
 performance (4); Israel and Soviet Union job performance (2);
 religion's influence on American life (2); December 7, 1941 (2); Pearl
 Harbor (3); serve in armed forces (3); forgive Japan for Pearl Harbor
 (1); Japan's aggressiveness (2); atomic bomb (1); relocation camps (1);
 economic threats to US (1); relationship between US and Japan (1).
