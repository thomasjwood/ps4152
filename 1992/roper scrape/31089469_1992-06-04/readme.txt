1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, June 4-5, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with a national adult sample of 978.
  
Approximate number of variables: 34
Topical Coverage: 
 Vote in three-way Presidential election (2); reason for supporting Ross
 Perot (1); vote for Perot and effects on other candidates (2);
 importance of President from major parties (1); Perot (12); effects of
 certain possible vice-presidential candidates on Perot's support (6).
