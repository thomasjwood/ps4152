1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Des Moines Register, December 2-5, 7-8, 1991 and based
 on telephone (landline only) interviews with 402 likely Democratic caucus
 participants in Iowa.
  
Approximate number of variables: 18
Topical Coverage: 
 Precinct caucus attendance (1); presidential candidate preferences in
 1992 presidential election (2); economy direction (2); financial
 situation (1); national healthcare (1).
