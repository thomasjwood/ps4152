1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by CBS News, September 22-27, 1992 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of 4653.
  
Approximate number of variables: 83
Topical Coverage: 
 1992 presidential campaign (11); presidential trial heats (4); Ross
 Perot (6); George Bush job performance (1).
