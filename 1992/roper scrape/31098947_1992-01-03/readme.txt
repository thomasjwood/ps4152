1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Washington Post, January 3-7, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with a national adult sample of 1002.
  
Approximate number of variables: 18
Topical Coverage: 
 Dan Quayle job performance (2); media treatment of Dan Quayle (1);
 Quayle as vice-presidential candidate (2); party identification (1).
