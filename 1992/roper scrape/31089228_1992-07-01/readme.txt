1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Employee Benefit Research Institute
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, July, 1992 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of 1001.
  
Approximate number of variables: 26
Topical Coverage: 
 Medicare and retirement health benefits (14).
