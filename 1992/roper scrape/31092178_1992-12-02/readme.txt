1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Applied Research Center, Georgia State University,
 December 2-15, 1992 and based on telephone interviews with an adult
 residents of Georgia sample of 773.
  
Approximate number of variables: 82
Topical Coverage: 
 Direction of Georgia (1); 1996 Summer Olympics (7); university system
 of Georgia (13); the Atlanta Project (1); environment (1); issues and
 the Georgia State Assembly (11); health care (4).
