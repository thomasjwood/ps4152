1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by CBS News, The New York Times and Tokyo Broadcasting
 System, December 7-9, 1992 and based on telephone interviews with a
 national adult sample of 1333.
  
Approximate number of variables: 56
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (2); opinion of political leaders (1); life
 satisfaction (1); economy (3); U.S.-Japan relations (7); Japan (3);
 expectations of the Clinton administration (4); U.S. troops in Somalia
 (5); voting record (1); voter registration (1); personal military
 record (1); party preference (2); political ideology (1).
