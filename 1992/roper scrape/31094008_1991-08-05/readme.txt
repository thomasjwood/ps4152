1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Star Tribune, August 5-20, 1991 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with 1041 adult residents of Minnesota.
  
Approximate number of variables: 41
Topical Coverage: 
 Crime (8); death penalty (6); sex crimes (27).
