1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, July 18-21, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1002.
  
Approximate number of variables: 79
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); problems facing the country (13);
 performance of political parties (2); Persian Gulf war (23); Mikhail
 Gorbachev (2); Soviet Union (8); labor unions (1); air traffic
 controllers strike (3); strikes (1); nuclear arms reduction treaty (2);
 drug use (4); volunteer programs for drug users (4).
