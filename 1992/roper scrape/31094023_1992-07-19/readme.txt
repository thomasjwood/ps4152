1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Star Tribune, July 19-21, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with 602 adult residents of Minnesota.
  
Approximate number of variables: 35
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (1); voting record (2); political interest (1);
 voting intention (10); evaluation of political leaders (5); candidates
 (3); political cynicism (2); party preference (2); ideology (1).
