1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: ABC News
 Conducted by Chilton Research Services, July 25-28, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1205.
  
Approximate number of variables: 95
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (6); economy/recession (3); health care/
 insurance  (14); Persian gulf war(6); Clarence Thomas (5); impression
 of other countries (7); Mikhail Gorbachev (2); US-Soviet Union
 relations (8); communism (2); US-Israel relations (6); 1992 election
 for House Representatives (2); AIDS (11).
