1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Cable News Network and Knight Ridder
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, August 28-September 2, 1992 and based
 on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1387.
  
Approximate number of variables: 86
Topical Coverage: 
 1992 presidential election (2); satisfaction with the way things are
 going in the U.S. (1); most important problem facing the country (1);
 standard of living (1); satisfaction with certain aspects of life (10);
 United States in decline (4); trust in institutions and people (4);
 government performance (9); taxes, government spending and the deficit
 (5); jobs and the economy (7); confidence in the government (4); crime
 (8); health care (7); poverty and homelessness (7).
