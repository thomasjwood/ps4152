1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Americans Talk Issues Foundation
 Conducted by The Telephone Center, November 7-20, 1993 and based on
 telephone (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of
 2015.
  
Approximate number of variables: 134
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (5); federal government (4); Clean Air Act
 (3); energy (19); economic competitiveness (18); environment (20);
 regulation (3); toxic wastes (14); taxes (27).
