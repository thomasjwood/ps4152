1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, March 13, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with a national adult sample of 513.
  
Approximate number of variables: 17
Topical Coverage: 
 National economy (6); Congressional job performance (3); term
 limitations in Congress (1); Congressional pay raise (1); check-
 bouncing controversy (6).
