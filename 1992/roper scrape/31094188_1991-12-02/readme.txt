1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Americans Talk Issues Foundation
 Conducted by The Telephone Center, December 2-15, 1993 and based on
 telephone (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of
 2010. There are 2 forms for this study located in Card 1, Column 4.
 Form A=1; Form B=2.
  
Approximate number of variables: 82
Topical Coverage: 
 Concern over energy efficiency (1); domestic or foreign pollution
 policies (1); foreign aid for environmental purposes (2); government
 regulation of fuel economy (19); convincing arguments for or against
 fuel efficiency (18); increasing oil taxes (2); convincing arguments
 for or against increased oil taxes (14); foreign oil as threat to
 national security (1); support for carbon tax (1); preferred type of
 energy tax (1); proposal to increase fuel efficiency (2); preferring
 rebates over tax increases (1); government standards for energy
 regulation (1); less objectionable fuel economy regulation (2).
