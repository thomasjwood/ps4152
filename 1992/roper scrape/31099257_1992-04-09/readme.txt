1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, April 9, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1250.
  
Approximate number of variables: 53
Topical Coverage: 
 Party identification (2); national situation (1); voter registration
 (1); voting intention (9); opinion of political leaders (3); Bush job
 performance (1); presidential candidates (11); economy (3); abortion
 (4); labor strikes (2).
