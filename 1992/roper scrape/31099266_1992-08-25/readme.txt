1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, August 25-30, 1992 and based
 on telephone interviews with 5500 adult residents of five counties:
 Contra Costa, California; DeKalb, Georgia; Montgomery, Ohio; St. Louis,
 Missouri; and Middlesex, New Jersey. 1,100 residents in each county.
  
Approximate number of variables: 32
Topical Coverage: 
 Party preference (1); national situation (1); voter registration (1);
 voting intentions (6); presidential candidates (6); George Bush job
 performance (1); opinion of political leaders (1); voting record (1);
 most important issue (1); economy (3); political parties (1).
