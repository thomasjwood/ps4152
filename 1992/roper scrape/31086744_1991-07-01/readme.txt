1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: ABC News / The Washington Post
 Conducted by Chilton Research Services, July 1, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 553.
  
Approximate number of variables: 28
Topical Coverage: 
 U.S. Supreme Court job performance (6); Clarence Thomas' nomination
 (11); abortion (2); John Sununu (4).
