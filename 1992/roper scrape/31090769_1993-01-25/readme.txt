1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by CBS News, January 25-27, 1993 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with 1022 adult residents of Los Angeles,
 California.
  
Approximate number of variables: 48
Topical Coverage: 
 General local situation (3); most important local problem (1); crime
 (6); racial relations (2); 1992 riots (5); Rodney King case (4); party
 preference (2); ideology (1).
