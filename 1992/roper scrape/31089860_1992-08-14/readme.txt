1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Atlanta Journal-Constitution, August 14-19, 1992 and
 based on telephone interviews with 1821 adult residents of twelve southern
 states.
  
Approximate number of variables: 29
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (1); voting intentions (3); national situation (1);
 George Bush job performance (3); presidential candidates (9); voting
 record (1); party preference (1); political ideology (1); church
 attendance (1).
