1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: USA Today
 Conducted by Gordon S. Black Corporation, August 22, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 617.
  
Approximate number of variables: 11
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); Mikhail Gorbachev vs. Boris Yeltsin
 (1); U.S. policy toward the Soviet Union (5).
