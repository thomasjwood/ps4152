1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Nightly Business Report News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, May 4-12, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with 435 executives of American companies with more
 than $1 million in sales or revenue.
  
Approximate number of variables: 20
Topical Coverage: 
 U.S. education system and job training (16).
