1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, October 24-27, 1991 and based on
 telephone (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of
 1003.
  
Approximate number of variables: 
Topical Coverage: 
 Cigarette smoking (7); ban on smoking (5); rights of smokers (4); David
 Duke (3).
