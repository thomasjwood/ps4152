1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Washington Post, January 13-27 (women), 25-29 (men), 1993
 and based on telephone interviews with 803 Current and former
 congressional staff. The sample includes 603 current and
 former congressional staff women and 200 current and former
 congressional staff men.
  
Approximate number of variables: 40
Topical Coverage: 
 Congressional staffer work (6); women and Congressional staff jobs (2);
 job satisfaction (1); sexual harassment (13); Senator Packwood (3).
