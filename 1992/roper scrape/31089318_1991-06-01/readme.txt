1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: U.S. Council for Energy Awareness (USCEA)
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, June, 1991 and based on telephone
 interviews with a national adult sample of 846.
  
Approximate number of variables: 12
Topical Coverage: 
 Importance of nuclear energy plants in meeting the nation's electricity
 needs (1); issuance of nuclear power plant licenses (1).
