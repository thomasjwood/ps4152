1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Roper Organization, February 8-22, 1992 and based on
 face to face interviews with a national adult sample of 1997.
  
Approximate number of variables: 52
Topical Coverage: 
 National economic situation (2); most important issues (1); U.S.-Russia
 relations (1); evaluation of companies (1); family life style (2);
 cable television (4); advertising (1); social problems (1); drinking
 (3); personal fears (1); personal health (4); shopping habits (8); food
 habits (2); party preference (1); ideology (1).
