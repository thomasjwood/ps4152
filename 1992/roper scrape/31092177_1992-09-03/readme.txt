1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Center for Urban Policy Research, September 3-20, 1992
 and based on telephone interviews with 811 adult residents of Georgia.
  
Approximate number of variables: 89
Topical Coverage: 
 Georgia State University (11); Georgia state flag (5); state lottery
 (1); 1992 presidential election (4); negative campaigning (7); child
 abuse (24).
