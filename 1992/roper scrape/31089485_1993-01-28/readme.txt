1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, January 28-29, 1993 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 774.
  
Approximate number of variables: 34
Topical Coverage: 
 Bill Clinton job performance (1); Hillary Clinton job performance (3);
 homosexuals in the military(3); US military in Bosnia (3); United
 Nations sanctions against Israel (1); federal budget deficit (6);
 economy (5).
