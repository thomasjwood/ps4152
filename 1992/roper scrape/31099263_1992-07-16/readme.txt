1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, July 16, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a registered voter sample of 500.
  
Approximate number of variables: 30
Topical Coverage: 
 Party preference (1); national situation (1); voter registration (1);
 voting intentions (4); political candidates (6); opinion of political
 leaders (1); Ross Perot withdrawal (5); political parties (1).
