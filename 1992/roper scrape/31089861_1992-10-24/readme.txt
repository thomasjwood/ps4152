1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: WSB-TV
 Conducted by The Atlanta Journal-Constitution, October 24-27, 1992 and
 based on telephone interviews with 1240 likely voters of twelve southern
 states.
  
Approximate number of variables: 41
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (1); voting intention (7); general situation (1);
 George Bush job performance (1); political leaders (2); political
 advertisements (13); voting record (1); party preference (1); political
 ideology (1).
