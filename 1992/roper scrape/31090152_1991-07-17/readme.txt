1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Associated Press
 Conducted by ICR Survey Research Group, July 17-21, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1002.
  
Approximate number of variables: 34
Topical Coverage: 
 U.S. military effort in the Persian Gulf (3); reasons for going to war
 with Iraq (5).
