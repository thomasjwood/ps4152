1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Des Moines Register, October 25- November 1, 1992 and
 based on telephone (landline only) interviews with a Likely voters of
 Iowa plus oversamples of the Second and Third Congressional Districts
 sample of 1255.
  
Approximate number of variables: 22
Topical Coverage: 
 1992 election (5); 1992 congressional election (2); Equal Rights
 amendment to the Iowa Constitution (1).
