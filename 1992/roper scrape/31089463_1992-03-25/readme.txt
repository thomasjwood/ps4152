1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, March 25-27, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with 511 national Black adults.
  
Approximate number of variables: 30
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); presidential election and Black issues
 (2); Black leaders and crime (6); problems facing Blacks (19).
