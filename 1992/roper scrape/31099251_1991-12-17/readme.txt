1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Clancy Shulman, December 17-22, 1991 and based
 on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1500.
  
Approximate number of variables: 134
Topical Coverage: 
 National situation (1); 1992 presidential election (19); 1992
 presidential candidates (12); Dan Quayle job performance (4); personal
 life and the presidency (8); 1992 presidential primaries (2); George
 Bush job performance (10); party that can better handle nation's
 problems (16); government and interest groups (4); middle class (7);
 class situation in the U.S. (5); problems facing the nation (25);
 homeless (4); John F. Kennedy assassination (3).
