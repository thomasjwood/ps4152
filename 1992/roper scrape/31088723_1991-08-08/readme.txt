1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, August 8-11, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1013.
  
Approximate number of variables: 41
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (4); Dan Quayle (5); Clarence Thomas (1);
 Robert Gates as CIA Director (1); foreign countries (8); situation in
 the Middle East (7). metric system (2).
