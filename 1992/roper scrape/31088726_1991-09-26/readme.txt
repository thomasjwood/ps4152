1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, September 26-29, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1005.
  
Approximate number of variables: 111
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (4); U.S. role in world affairs (1); Israel
 and the Middle East (4); eating habits (60); nutritional information
 (25); cooking (4).
