1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Employee Benefit Research Institute
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, August, 1991 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of 1000.

Topical Coverage: 
 Long-term health care (14).
