1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Washington Post, August 29-September 1, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 804.
  
Approximate number of variables: 42
Topical Coverage: 
 U.S. defense spending and changes in Soviet bloc countries (4); Mikhail
 Gorbachev (5); communism vs. democracy (7).
