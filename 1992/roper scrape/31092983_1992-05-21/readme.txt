1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Los Angeles Times, May 21-22, 1992 and based on telephone
 interviews with 725 adult residents of the city of San Diego.
  
Approximate number of variables: 51
Topical Coverage: 
 Satisfaction with the community (1); 1992 San Diego mayoral primary
 (3); city government (1); impressions of candidates (10); Proposition F
 (3); Proposition A (3); gubernatorial election (1).
