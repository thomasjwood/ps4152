1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Center for Urban Policy Research, June 3-15, 1992 and
 based on telephone interviews with 813 adult residents of Georgia.
  
Approximate number of variables: 57
Topical Coverage: 
 Voting preferences (5); presidential candidates in the 1992
 presidential election (2); Georgia state lottery (1); media election
 coverage (4); MARTA (2); abortion (15); environment (9); 1996 Summer
 Olympics (8); the Atlanta Project (4).
