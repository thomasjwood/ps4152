1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Time Magazine and Cable News Network
 Conducted by Yankelovich Partners, Inc., January 22-25, 1993 and based
 on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1800.
  
Approximate number of variables: 73
Topical Coverage: 
 Impressions of political leaders (3); public education (6); Judeo-
 Christian principles and secular humanism (4); morals (10); homosexuals
 (3); abortion (2); religion (8).
