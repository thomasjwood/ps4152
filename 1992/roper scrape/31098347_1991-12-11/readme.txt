1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: USA Today
 Conducted by Gordon S. Black Corporation, December 11, 1991 and based
 on telephone interviews with 605 national adults--who know something about
 the William Kennedy Smith trial.
  
Approximate number of variables: 28
Topical Coverage: 
 William Kennedy Smith trial: press coverage (4); Smith case and verdict
 (4); effects on reporting rape (1).
