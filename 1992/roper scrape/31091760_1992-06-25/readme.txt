1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 June 25-July 1, 1992 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews
 with 675 adult residents of Connecticut.
  
Approximate number of variables: 86
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (2); party preference (1); political
 ideology (1); presidential candidates (14); voting intentions (5);
 union membership (1); voter registration (1); national situation (3);
 economic situation (3); state political leaders (10); source of news
 (1); political interest (2); evaluation of media (4); summer Olympics
 (9); environmental health (11).
