1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Associated Press
 Conducted by ICR Survey Research Group, September 27-October 1, 1991
 and based on telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1014.

Topical Coverage: 
 Medical ethics/government (2); medical ethics/insurance companies (1);
 medical ethics/morality (4).
