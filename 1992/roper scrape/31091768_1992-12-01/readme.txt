1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 December 1-7, 1992 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews
 with an adult residents of Connecticut sample of 500.
  
Approximate number of variables: 88
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (2); national situation (5); economic
 situation (10); U.S. role in world affairs (9); homosexuality (11);
 Governor Lowell Weicker job performance (1); state spending (4);
 religious leaders and politics (9); hockey (4).
