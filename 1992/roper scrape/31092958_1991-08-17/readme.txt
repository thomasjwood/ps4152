1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by Los Angeles Times, August 17, 1991 and based on telephone
 interviews with 1063 adult residents of San Diego County.
  
Approximate number of variables: 49
Topical Coverage: 
 Most important problem facing San Diego county (1); confidence in fire
 department (1); confidence in police/county sheriff (1); confidence in
 elected officials (1); law enforcement in community (8); San Diego city
 police department (1); San Diego county sheriff's department (1).
