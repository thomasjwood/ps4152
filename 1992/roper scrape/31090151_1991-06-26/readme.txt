1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Associated Press
 Conducted by ICR Survey Research Group, June 26-30, 1991 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1003.
  
Approximate number of variables: 31
Topical Coverage: 
 National economic situation (2); national banking system (1); family
 finances (2).
