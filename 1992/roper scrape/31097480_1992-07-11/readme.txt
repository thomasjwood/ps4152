1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Roper Organization, July 11-18, 1992 and based on face
 to face interviews with a national adult sample of 1992. Form location
 is in column 5:  Form X='1'+'2'; Form Y= '3'+'4'
  
Approximate number of variables: 190
Topical Coverage: 
 Protection from crime (10); sexual preference relevancy for certain
 occupations (16); talking with children about issues (26); connotations
 of phrases (21); consumer choices (16); satisfaction with life (13).
