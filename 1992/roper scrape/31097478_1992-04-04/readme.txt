1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
 Conducted by The Roper Organization, April 4-11, 1992 and based on face
 to face interviews with a national adult sample of 1990. There are 2
 forms for this study located in column 5.  Form X=1 & 2; Form Y=3 & 4.
  
Approximate number of variables: 195
Topical Coverage: 
 Government agencies (14); countries which have become military/economic
 forces (23); banning products because of health risks (16); American
 vs. foreign made products (22).
