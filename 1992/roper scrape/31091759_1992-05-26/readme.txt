1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: The Hartford Courant
 Conducted by Institute for Social Inquiry, University of Connecticut,
 May 26-June 6, 1992 and based on telephone (landline only) interviews
 with 500 adult residents of Connecticut. There are two
 forms for this study; 1) Form A and 2) Form C.
  
Approximate number of variables: 88
Topical Coverage: 
 George Bush job performance (1); 1992 presidential candidates (15);
 1992 presidential election (3); state political leaders (12); economic
 situation (1); state income tax (2); automobile driving (10); race
 relations (7); Los Angeles riots (2); urban assistance (2); state
 legislators (4); public education (13).
