1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Employee Benefit Research Institute
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, February 1992 and based on telephone
 (landline only) interviews with a national adult sample of 1001.
  
Approximate number of variables: 25
Topical Coverage: 
 Support for specific changes in health care system (2); health services
 decisions (5); choice in health insurance (4).
