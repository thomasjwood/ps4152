1. STUDY DESCRIPTION:
Methodology: 
Survey by: Newsweek Magazine
 Conducted by Gallup Organization, July 9-10, 1992 and based on
 telephone interviews with a national adult sample of 1010.
  
Approximate number of variables: 22
Topical Coverage: 
 Voter registration (1); party preference (2); voting record (1); voting
 intention (4); presidential candidates (9).
